This is the shortest post in the series, because getting started is genuinely simple. You don't need to write code, configure servers, or learn new tools.

## What You Need

- A willingness to spend 5 minutes of your time
- An IT person or internal developer (if you have one) for the initial setup
- A desire to stop worrying about surprise AI bills

That's it.

## Step 1: Tell Your IT Person About agent-gov

Copy this message and send it to the person who manages your technology:

> *"Hi [Name], I'd like us to try agent-gov — it's a free, open-source tool that puts spending limits and guardrails on our AI agents. It installs in about 5 minutes and sits between our agents and the tools they use. It prevents cost blowouts like the $30K surprise bills we've been reading about. Can you set it up? The instructions are at github.com/sschelliah2026-source/agent-gov. Happy to walk through it together."*

That's all you need to write. Your IT person will know what to do from there.

## Step 2: Decide Your Budgets

While your IT person sets things up, think about what makes sense for your business:

- **What's a reasonable daily budget for each AI agent?** Start conservatively — you can always increase it later.
- **Which tools should your agents avoid?** Think about expensive premium models or tools for tasks that don't need them.
- **Who should approve expensive actions?** Pick one person (you, a manager, a finance lead) to be the approval contact.

## Step 3: Set Your First Policies

Once agent-gov is running, you'll be able to set rules like these — all in plain language, no code needed:

- "Block any tool that costs more than $5 per call"
- "Warn me when an agent uses 80% of its daily budget"
- "Ask for approval before exporting data"
- "Pause any agent that exceeds its daily budget"

Think of these as the employee handbook for your digital workforce.

## Step 4: Review and Adjust

After a week, check the dashboard. You'll see:

- Which agents spent the most (and whether that surprised you)
- Which tools are costing you money
- How much the policies saved you
- Whether any budgets need adjusting

Chances are, you'll find at least one agent doing something you didn't expect — buying premium services for routine tasks, or running more calls than you thought. That's the whole point of having visibility.

## Still Not Sure?

Here's a simple test: ask your IT person one question — "What are our AI agents actually spending?"

If they can't give you a clear answer with a per-agent, per-tool breakdown, you need agent-gov.

The free version takes 5 minutes to set up. If it saves you from even ONE surprise bill, it's already paid for itself a thousand times over.
