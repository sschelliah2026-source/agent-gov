Your AI assistant just cost you $30,000 — and it was only trying to help.

Here's the reality most businesses don't realize yet.

AI agents are fundamentally different from the software you've used before. A traditional app processes one request at a time. An AI agent can branch, loop, call external APIs, retry, escalate, and spawn sub-agents — all in a single "task."

The execution path is a tree, not a line.

And AI agents don't understand the value of money. When you tell one to "analyze our competitors," it might run a $0.03 search, then a $0.35 summary, then decide that's not polished enough and upgrade to a premium model at $2.50 per call — then regenerate three times because it wants to get it right.

Suddenly one "simple" task costs $13.39. Multiply by hundreds of tasks.

This isn't a technical problem. It's a governance problem.

Three things every business running AI agents needs:

1. VISIBILITY — Know exactly what each agent spends, on which tools, minute by minute
2. BOUNDARIES — Each agent gets a daily budget. Hard cap. No exceptions.
3. ACCOUNTABILITY — An audit trail of every action, every cost, every block

The good news? You don't need an engineering team to set this up. The technology exists, it's open-source, and it takes about 5 minutes to deploy.

We built agent-gov to solve exactly this problem — a lightweight governance layer that sits between your AI agents and the tools they use. It tracks every cent, enforces budgets, and auto-pauses agents that overspend.

Your AI agents should be productive. They shouldn't be writing checks you didn't authorize.
