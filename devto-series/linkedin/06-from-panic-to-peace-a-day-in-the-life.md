A Day in the Life with AI Agent Governance — Meet Priya

Priya runs a busy clinic in Chennai. Three doctors, two receptionists, hundreds of patients. She uses AI agents to manage patient records, appointments, and follow-ups.

Six months ago, she had a panic attack checking her monthly cloud bill. Today, she doesn't even think about it.

Here's what changed.

☕ 8:30 AM — Morning Check

Priya opens her dashboard while sipping chai:

• Reception Agent: ✅ $4.20 / $30 budget
• Doctor Agent: ✅ $2.10 / $50 budget
• Analytics Agent: ✅ $1.05 / $20 budget
• Reminder Agent: ⏸ PAUSED ($18.50 — hit its limit yesterday)

Total: $7.35 spent. Everything under control.

The Reminder Agent hit its $20 budget yesterday. It auto-paused. It'll auto-resume at midnight. Priya doesn't need to do a thing.

🆔 10:15 AM — The Blocked Call

The receptionist calls: "Patient registration isn't working."

Priya checks the activity log. The agent tried to use a premium OCR tool ($12.00/call) to read a patient's ID. The proxy immediately blocked it — policy said "max $5 per tool call." The agent fell back to standard OCR ($0.80).

Saved $11.20 on a single call. The receptionist didn't even notice the switch.

📱 2:30 PM — Approval Request

Her phone buzzes: "Analytics Agent wants to export all patient data for monthly report. Cost: $5.00. Policy: require approval for data exports."

Priya taps Approve. The agent proceeds. The report lands in her email.

This is accountability without friction — the agent required permission for a sensitive action, got it, and continued.

⚠️ 4:45 PM — Budget Warning

Another notification: Reception Agent has used 85% of its daily budget.

Priya notes it. Doesn't panic. The agent will auto-pause at $30 if needed, and budget resets at midnight.

📊 6:30 PM — End of Day Review

Daily summary:
• Total spent: $34.18
• Saved by policies: $28.70
• Violations blocked: 4
• Approvals: 1 (approved)

Month end? Her bill dropped from $3,200 to $847. Annual savings: $28,236.

The real win isn't the savings — it's the peace of mind. Priya knows exactly what her AI agents are doing, what they're spending, and that they can't go rogue.

She sleeps through the night.

The technology is ready. The tools are free and open-source. The question is whether you want to find out what your AI costs the hard way.
