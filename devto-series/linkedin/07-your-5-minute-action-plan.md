Your 5-Minute Action Plan for AI Agent Governance

No code. No servers. No jargon. Here's exactly how to get control of your AI costs.

📋 Step 1: Ask One Question

Send this message to your IT person or developer:

"Can you tell me, right now, exactly what our AI agents spent today — broken down by agent and by tool?"

If they can't give you a clear answer with a per-agent, per-tool breakdown, you need a governance layer.

📋 Step 2: Decide Your Budgets

While your team looks into tools, decide:
• What's a reasonable daily budget for each AI agent? (Start conservative — you can always increase)
• Which tools should your agents avoid? (Premium models for routine tasks = waste)
• Who should approve expensive actions? (Manager, finance lead, or you)

📋 Step 3: Deploy agent-gov

agent-gov is a free, open-source governance layer that installs in 5 minutes. Your IT person can do it without any special infrastructure:

pip install agent-gov-saas
agent-gov start

That's it. Now every agent call goes through a proxy that enforces budgets, tracks costs, and auto-pauses overspending agents.

Set your first policy:
• "Block any tool over $5 per call"
• "Warn me at 80% budget usage"
• "Require approval for data exports"

📋 Step 4: Review After One Week

Check the dashboard. You'll likely find at least one agent doing something you didn't expect — buying premium services for routine tasks, or running more calls than you thought.

That's the whole point of visibility.
